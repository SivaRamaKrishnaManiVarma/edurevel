Alternative Learning Pathways

Balancing college classes, university timetables, and career preparation? EduRevel offers two flexible tracks tailor-made for students currently in college, designed to fit seamlessly around your academic schedule.

Open Access Principle

Anyone Can Join — Absolutely No Technical Degree Barrier

You do not need a B.Tech degree or prior programming background to enroll. Our training is open to all students across every department, stream, and degree program (engineering, computer applications, science, commerce, and arts). We start right from the foundational basics and mentor every student step-by-step into real software and AI development.

Two Flexible Formats

Choose the Track That Fits Your College Schedule

Live Evening Track 4 Months

Evening Live Track (6 to 8 PM)

Daily live classes after your college hours with real-time mentor interaction.

Tailored for college students who finish daytime university lectures and want dedicated, focused evening learning without disturbing daytime academics.

  • Timings: 6:00 PM – 8:00 PM — Timed comfortably after college lectures and commute.
  • Live Interactive Classes: Taught live by corporate industry professionals with immediate doubt solving.
  • Live Coding & Labs: Hands-on evening coding labs where mentors guide you through project milestones.
  • Class Recordings Provided: Never miss a class—full recordings available if you have college exams or events.
